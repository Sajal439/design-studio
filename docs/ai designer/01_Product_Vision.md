# Product Vision: HomeCraft AI
**The Renovation Operating System for Goel Traders**

---

## 1. Executive Summary
HomeCraft AI is a design-to-delivery renovation platform that integrates generative AI, instant estimation, and CRM workflow. It empowers homeowners in Haryana (Karnal, Panipat, Kurukshetra, Ambala) to visualize their spaces, estimate costs in real-time, purchase materials directly from Goel Traders, and track installation progress. Simultaneously, it equips the Goel Traders sales and design teams with leads, CRM tools, and analytics.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
The home renovation market in India is highly fragmented, opaque, and friction-filled:
- **For Homeowners:**
  - **Visualization Gap:** Difficulty imagining how laminates, handles, and modular layouts look in their own rooms.
  - **Estimation Uncertainty:** Contractors frequently inflate quotes or give opaque, flat-rate pricing that escalates during construction.
  - **Fragmented Procurement:** Sourcing plywood, hardware, and laminates from multiple vendors leads to quality inconsistencies and delays.
- **For Goel Traders:**
  - **Inbound Lead Friction:** The design gallery on goeltraders.in drives inspiration but requires manual follow-ups to convert to sales.
  - **High Estimator Churn:** Users run calculations on the simple estimator but lack a way to save their projects, causing drop-offs.
  - **Inefficient Design-to-Quote Cycle:** Designers spend days drafting CAD drawings before the sales team can compile a material quotation.

---

## 3. Value Proposition
- **Visualize Instantly:** Homeowners upload photos of their actual empty or semi-furnished rooms and generate photorealistic, styled renders in seconds.
- **Exact Material Translation:** Renders are analyzed by AI to recommend actual laminates, boards, and hardware from Goel Traders' inventory (Action Tesa, Hettich, Advance Laminates).
- **Guaranteed Pricing:** Converts designs into structured quotations, breaking down sheets of BWR plywood, soft-close hinges, and labor costs.
- **Unified Delivery:** Unifies the client, the site designer, and the installer on a single project board.

---

## 4. User Personas

### A. The Homeowner (Rohan, 32)
- **Context:** Newly bought 3BHK apartment in Sector 32, Karnal. Busy corporate professional.
- **Goals:** Wants a modern modular kitchen and wardrobes. Needs transparent pricing and high-quality materials.
- **Pain Points:** Hard to find time to meet designers; suspicious of local contractors' pricing; wants to select brand-name materials (Action Tesa HDHMR, Hettich channels).

### B. The Showroom Sales Executive (Amit, 28)
- **Context:** Works at the Goel Traders Karnal showroom.
- **Goals:** Convert walk-in visitors and website leads into material orders.
- **Pain Points:** Spends hours explaining the difference between PVC edge banding and laminate grades; manually calculates quotes based on hand-drawn sketches.

### C. The Site Designer (Priya, 26)
- **Context:** In-house or freelance interior designer partner for Goel Traders.
- **Goals:** Create functional layouts and assist clients in styling.
- **Pain Points:** Client repeatedly changes mind about colors/laminates; redrafting in CAD takes hours.

### D. The Administrator (Goel Traders Management)
- **Context:** Owners of Goel Traders.
- **Goals:** Track revenue pipeline, catalog popularity, stock usage, and employee performance.
- **Pain Points:** Hard to track which designs convert; lacks visibility into lead-to-installation pipelines.

---

## 5. Strategic Goals & Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Lead Generation:** Increase monthly online consultations by 150% via interactive AI features.
- **User Retention:** Keep clients on-platform by allowing them to save multiple project versions.
- **Sales Conversion:** Shorten the design-to-quotation cycle from 5 days to under 15 minutes.
- **Inventory Turn:** Direct user selections toward authorized partner brands (Action Tesa, Advance Laminates, Dorset, Hettich).
